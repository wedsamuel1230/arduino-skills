# Print and Protection Guidance

Use this reference when the user asks about print settings, durability, or
environmental resistance.

## Wall Thickness

- light indoor use: start around 2 mm
- heavier use or larger panels: increase thickness and add ribs if needed
- thin walls around screw bosses fail early; reinforce locally

## Clearances

- add fit tolerance for printed parts
- leave extra room around USB ports, cable strain relief, and headers
- reserve vertical clearance for solder joints and stacked modules

## Material Selection

- PLA for quick indoor prototypes
- PETG for better heat and outdoor tolerance
- ABS or ASA when higher temperature or UV resistance matters and the print
  setup supports it

## Ventilation

Add ventilation when the enclosure contains:

- regulators that dissipate heat
- radios with thermal constraints
- batteries in warm environments

Place vents where they do not compromise mounting or splash protection.

## Waterproofing

- Printed parts alone do not guarantee an IP rating.
- Use gaskets, cable glands, and sealed fastener paths when water resistance is
  required.
- State environmental limits explicitly instead of implying full waterproofing.
